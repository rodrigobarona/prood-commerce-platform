# EU and Portuguese obligations

Which EU and Portuguese laws apply to Búzios e Tartarugas, Lda. ("Prood"), in two roles: **(a)** as a provider of a hosted commerce platform, and **(b)** as the processor and provider for merchants, who carry their own duties as sellers. Where a law binds merchants and not Prood, the table says what Prood gives them and what they must do.

> **Every row needs counsel.** This is a first map drafted on 2026-11-07 with AI assistance, from the code and the public texts, and updated the same day after the public texts were revised following an independent AI review (not a lawyer's review). It is not legal advice, and it does not say Prood "complies" with anything (`_docs/BRAND.md` bans "GDPR compliant"). Where an article number or Portuguese statute is uncertain, the row says **verify** instead of guessing. Statute numbers marked verify are from memory.

**Assumptions that drive several answers, all unverified.** Prood has fewer than 10 people and turnover or balance sheet total of at most EUR 2 million (micro-enterprise) or at most 50 people and EUR 10 million (small enterprise, Recommendation 2003/361/EC). The owner confirms the real figures ([open items](./open-items.md)). Prood contracts only with businesses (`/terms`, "The contract"). Paid plans cannot be bought yet, and billing is in Stripe test mode.

**Status words.** *In place*: there is a control and a place to check it. *Partly*: a control exists with a named gap. *Gap*: required or advisable and missing. *Not applicable*. *Verify*: counsel or the owner must decide. "Ships" means in the uncommitted working tree on 2026-11-07 (see the [README](./README.md)).

**What matters most.**

1. Data Act switching rights are the clearest platform duty. The terms and the new store export cover them, but the export leaves out images, and the Free plan has no API route to the data.
2. The DPA depends on "no signature needed" acceptance. Done on 2026-11-07: the register form has a required box that links the Terms, the DPA and the Privacy policy, and the version and time are stored (`user.terms_version`, `user.terms_accepted_at`). Whether tick-box acceptance is enough is for counsel.
3. DSA hosting duties (contacts, notice and action, statements of reasons) are met by a mailbox and a few sentences. The text now sets out what a notice must contain, says Prood confirms receipt and tells the reporter what it decided, promises a statement of reasons to the Customer, and names English and Portuguese for the contact point. No notice register, no statement-of-reasons template with the Art. 17(3) elements.
4. P2B Regulation: whether it applies is open. If it does, `/terms` no longer promises to name mediators: it offers mediation on request with a mediator both sides agree on, which may not satisfy Art. 12 (two named mediators: verify).
5. Accessibility: Prood's likely micro-enterprise exemption does not protect merchants that are not micro-enterprises.
6. The AI Act does not make Prood a provider of an AI system, but `autonomous` agents and the transparency rules deserve a decision.
