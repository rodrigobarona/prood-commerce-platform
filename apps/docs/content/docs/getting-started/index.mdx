You can have a store online in about an hour, plus however long your domain's DNS takes. There's nothing to install.

## Open your store

1. [Create your account](https://prood.com/register). You'll give your name, a store name, a store address, your email and a password. **Create account and store** does the rest.
2. Your store is live straight away. You choose your store's prood.app address when you sign up. If it's taken, we add a few characters to the end. You'll find it under **Domains**.
3. Go to **Products** and choose **Add product**. Set its status to **Active** so customers can see it.
4. Go to **Payments** and connect your Stripe, Easypay or Ifthenpay account.
5. Share your address, or [add your own domain](/docs/apps/dashboard/domains).

The [merchant onboarding guide](/docs/guides/merchant-onboarding) covers each step in detail, including a test order.
