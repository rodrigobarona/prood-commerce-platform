**Team** shows who can work on this store. Everyone signs in with their own account.

| Feature | Availability |
| --- | --- |
| Team invites | From Grow. Free has one seat, the owner. |

Paid plans can't be bought yet. Running several client stores? Email us at hello@prood.com.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything. The person who created the store |
| Admin | Run the store: products, orders, payment accounts, domains and API keys. Invite and remove people |
| Member | See the store's data. They can't change it |

## Invite someone

Under **Invite a teammate**, enter their email and choose **Admin** or **Member**. They get an email, and access to this store's dashboard once they accept. Pending invitations are listed until they're accepted, and you can cancel one there.

## More than one store

Each store has its own team. If you belong to several, the store switcher at the top of the sidebar moves between them.
