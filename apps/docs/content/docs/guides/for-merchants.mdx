Prood is for anyone who wants to sell online without assembling a stack of separate tools. This page says what works today and what's still to come.

## What you get on day one

| Capability | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Create a store | Live |
| A prood.app address you choose when you sign up | Live |
| One custom domain on Free, with HTTPS set up for you | Live |
| Products with prices, descriptions and an in-stock switch | Live |
| Customer storefront: browse, cart, checkout | Live |
| Orders and customers in the dashboard | Live |
| Stripe: cards, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay where you've enabled them | Live |
| Easypay or Ifthenpay: Multibanco, MB WAY and cards | Live |
| Team invites | From Grow. Free has one seat, the owner. |
| API keys for your own code | Live |

You choose your store's prood.app address when you sign up. If it's taken, we add a few characters to the end.

Shipping is fixed for now: Standard 15.00 in your currency (5 to 7 days), Express 35.00 (1 to 2 days). Setting your own rates is coming later.

Prices are what you enter. Prood doesn't calculate VAT or issue invoices yet.

## Coming later

- Paid plan billing
- Uploading product photos in the dashboard
- Product variants, categories and stock counts in the dashboard
- Coupons you can create
- VAT calculation and invoices
- Returns, terms and privacy pages
- Analytics
- Fine-grained member permissions

## Your first hour

1. [Register](https://dashboard.prood.com/register) on the dashboard.
2. Add a few [products](/docs/apps/dashboard/products).
3. Connect a [payment account](/docs/apps/dashboard/integrations).
4. Share your prood.app address, or [add a custom domain](/docs/apps/dashboard/domains).
5. Place a test order and confirm it shows up in the dashboard.

For the full checklist, see [Merchant onboarding](/docs/guides/merchant-onboarding).

## Plans

Prices are on [prood.com/pricing](https://prood.com/pricing). Every plan includes a custom domain, and Prood charges by plan, not by sale.

| Plan | Price | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | €0 | 1 custom domain, 50 products, 100 orders a month, 1 seat |
| **Grow** | €29 a month | Agent Auth, the full API, 3 seats, 3 custom domains, 1,000 orders a month |
| **Scale** | €79 a month | Unlimited products and orders, 10 seats, priority support |
| **Agency** | €199 a month | Unlimited products and orders, dedicated support |

Paid plans can't be bought yet. Every store starts on Free, and Free limits apply today. Running several client stores? Email us at hello@prood.com. See [Plans and limits](/docs/billing/plans).
